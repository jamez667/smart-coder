Scope and limitations

DELIBERATELY NARROW. This pack covers only the technical safeguards of the HIPAA Security Rule as they surface in source code, and even then indirectly. Not covered, because no repository can evidence them: the entire administrative safeguard set (§164.308 — risk analysis, sanction policy, workforce clearance, security awareness training, incident procedures, contingency plan, evaluation), the entire physical safeguard set (§164.310 — facility access, workstation use, device and media controls), Business Associate Agreements (§164.314), and policy documentation and retention (§164.316). The prior question this tool cannot answer: **is ePHI in scope for this repository at all?** Encryption is an addressable — not required — implementation specification, meaning a covered entity may document why an alternative is reasonable. A clean result here is engineering hygiene, never a compliance determination. Consult counsel and a qualified assessor. This pack is a starting point for a technical conversation, not an assessment.

164.312(e)(1)gapTransmission security

Implement technical security measures to guard against unauthorized access to electronic protected health information being transmitted over an electronic communications network. Encryption is an addressable implementation specification.

all-of: worst of 2 check(s) is gap

Remediation: Enforce TLS on every endpoint that may carry ePHI and remove any code path that disables certificate verification. If encryption is not implemented, document the addressable-specification rationale.

164.312(a)(2)(iv)unknownEncryption and decryption of ePHI at rest

Implement a mechanism to encrypt and decrypt electronic protected health information. This is an addressable implementation specification: a covered entity may document why an alternative measure is reasonable and appropriate.

all-of: worst of 2 check(s) is unknown

Evidence to obtain
  • Data inventory or data flow diagrams explicitly mapping where ePHI is stored across all environments.
  • Cloud console exports or configuration screenshots showing at-rest encryption (e.g., AWS KMS, Azure Storage Service Encryption) enabled on those specific ePHI datastores.
  • Documented risk assessment and formal management approval if an alternative to encryption was implemented for any ePHI store.

Usually held by: Security Lead / Cloud Infrastructure Team

What an auditor will probe: Show me how you verify that every new database or storage bucket that receives ePHI has encryption enabled by default, and how you manage the lifecycle of those encryption keys.

Obtain: Enable storage encryption for any datastore holding ePHI, or document the addressable rationale. Replace deprecated cryptographic primitives.

164.308unknownAdministrative safeguards

Security management process (including the required risk analysis and risk management), assigned security responsibility, workforce security, information access management, security awareness and training, security incident procedures, contingency plan, and periodic evaluation.

all-of: worst of 1 check(s) is unknown

Evidence to obtain
  • The most recent, formally documented Security Risk Analysis (SRA) report.
  • Risk management action plan with corresponding ticket histories (e.g., Jira) showing active remediation of identified risks.
  • LMS exports showing dated security awareness training completion records for all employees.
  • Meeting minutes or after-action reports from the most recent incident response tabletop exercise.

Usually held by: Compliance Officer / HR

What an auditor will probe: Walk me through your most recent risk analysis and show me the actual project plans or tickets where you tracked the identified vulnerabilities through to remediation.

Obtain: Provide the risk analysis, workforce clearance records, training completion, incident procedures and contingency plan. The risk analysis is the foundational required specification — begin there.

164.314unknownOrganizational requirements — business associate contracts

A covered entity must obtain satisfactory assurances, documented through a written business associate agreement, that each business associate will appropriately safeguard the ePHI it creates, receives, maintains or transmits.

all-of: worst of 1 check(s) is unknown

Evidence to obtain
  • A vendor inventory matrix explicitly identifying which third-party services create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI.
  • Fully executed, countersigned Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) for every vendor identified in the matrix (e.g., AWS, SaaS providers).
  • Dated records of annual vendor security reviews or audits.

Usually held by: Legal / Privacy Officer

What an auditor will probe: Show me your procurement gatekeeping process that ensures a BAA is fully executed before any employee is technically able to upload ePHI to a new SaaS tool.

Obtain: Provide executed BAAs for every business associate, including cloud and SaaS providers.

Evidence to obtain
