Scope and limitations

DELIBERATELY NARROW — and GDPR is the framework where that matters most. GDPR governs the lawfulness of personal data processing. This pack can evidence a small part of Article 32 (security of processing) as it appears in code. It cannot evidence anything about: * lawful basis and consent (Art. 6, 7) * data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, portability (Art. 12-23) * records of processing activities (Art. 30) * data protection impact assessments (Art. 35) * the Data Protection Officer (Art. 37-39) * international transfers and their safeguards (Ch. V) * breach notification within 72 hours (Art. 33, 34) * processor contracts (Art. 28) Those are the substance of GDPR. A repository holds none of them. Article 32 itself requires measures "appropriate to the risk", which depends on what personal data is processed and how sensitive it is — questions this tool cannot answer. A passing result is engineering hygiene, not GDPR compliance, and must never be presented as the latter. Consult a data protection specialist.
