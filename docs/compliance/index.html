This automated source-code scan of the smart-coder project assessed 111 controls across 10 compliance frameworks. We evaluate controls based on where the evidence physically lives, leading with domains the repository can directly settle. In the Code domain, out of 36 controls, 20 passed, 9 are gaps, and 7 are unknown. In the Infrastructure domain, out of 37 controls, 13 passed, 6 are gaps, and 18 are unknown.

Resolving the outstanding gaps provides high leverage because several findings appear in multiple frameworks. The most critical gap is plaintext transport to a public host, which affects three frameworks. We also identified high-severity gaps for automated dependency vulnerability scanning and a lack of application logging, each appearing in three frameworks. Missing signed releases and build provenance creates a high-severity gap in two frameworks. Addressing these specific items will clear multiple requirements at once.

Across the scan, 61 controls are unknown. An unknown result is not a failure; it simply means the evidence cannot be determined from source code. This is expected for the Organizational domain, where all 35 controls are unknown because the evidence lives in HR systems, vendor contracts, and board minutes rather than this repository. The Documentation domain contains 3 controls, where 2 passed and 1 is unknown. Frameworks heavily reliant on governance, such as the EU regulatory cluster at 20 percent determinacy, naturally show low visibility in a code scan because their requirements are organizational.

How to read this

Determinacy is the number that matters. It is the share of in-scope controls the tool could decide either way. A low figure does not mean poor security — it means most of that framework is organizational (board oversight, vendor contracts, training records, incident procedures) and simply is not visible in source code.

Each framework's page states, in its own scope note, exactly what it cannot assess. That honesty is the point: a report claiming broad coverage of a governance framework from a code scan would be misleading.
