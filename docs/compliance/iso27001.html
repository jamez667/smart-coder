This is not a compliance attestation. It is the output of an automated scan of source code, showing what could and could not be evidenced from a repository. Most of any framework is organizational and cannot be assessed this way — those controls are reported as unknown, which is a statement about the tool's visibility, not about the organization. Certification requires an accredited auditor.

File paths, line numbers and evidence excerpts are withheld from this published version.

Scope and limitations

This pack inspects source-controlled artifacts only. It cannot observe running infrastructure, identity provider configuration, physical security, HR records, supplier contracts, or management review minutes. Annex A defines 93 controls across four themes (organizational, people, physical, technological). This pack evidences roughly twenty — almost entirely from the technological theme. The people and physical themes yield nothing from a repository and are declared undeterminable, not omitted. A passing result is evidence toward a control, never an attestation of conformity. Certification requires an accredited auditor.
