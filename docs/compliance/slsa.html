Scope and limitations

This pack inspects source-controlled build definitions. It cannot observe the build platform itself, signing key custody, or the runtime integrity of the builder — and SLSA levels are ultimately properties of that platform. What this pack CAN evidence: that a build requests provenance, that artifacts are signed, that dependencies are pinned and inventoried, and that the build is reproducible in intent. What it CANNOT evidence: that the builder is isolated, that provenance is unforgeable, or that keys are held securely. Treat a passing result as "the repository asks for the right things". Level attestation requires verifying the platform.

SLSA-B-L2gapBuild L2 — hosted build platform with signed provenance

The build runs on a hosted platform that generates and signs provenance. This prevents a developer from forging provenance on their own machine, though it does not yet protect against a compromised build itself.

all-of: worst of 2 check(s) is gap

Remediation: Move builds to a hosted CI platform and sign the provenance (keyless signing via Sigstore is the usual route).

SLSA-B-L1unknownBuild L1 — provenance exists and the build is scripted

The build process is fully scripted and automated, and generates provenance describing how the artifact was built. Provenance at L1 need not be tamper-proof; it must merely exist and be available to consumers.

weighted: 2.0/5.0 observable = 40% (pass>=60%, gap<30%)

Obtain: Define the build in a CI workflow and emit a provenance attestation (slsa-github-generator, or in-toto/cosign attest in another platform).

SLSA-B-L3unknownBuild L3 — hardened builds, non-falsifiable provenance

The build platform runs each build in an isolated environment and prevents secret material used to sign provenance from being accessible to user-defined build steps, making provenance non-falsifiable.

all-of: worst of 2 check(s) is unknown

Obtain: Use a reusable trusted builder (e.g. slsa-github-generator) rather than signing inside your own job, and pin all actions to immutable digests.
