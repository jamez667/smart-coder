Scope and limitations

DELIBERATELY THIN, and covering three regulations at once because each has only a handful of source-visible obligations. NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555): cybersecurity risk-management measures with management-body accountability, and incident reporting within 24/72 hours. Almost entirely governance and process. DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554): ICT risk management for financial entities, incident classification and reporting, digital operational resilience testing (including threat-led penetration testing), and oversight of critical ICT third-party providers via contractual registers. Entirely institutional. EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): risk classification, conformity assessment, technical documentation, data governance, human oversight and transparency for high-risk systems. Determined by INTENDED PURPOSE, which no scanner can infer from code. This pack evidences the engineering hygiene these regimes assume and flags whether AI components are present at all. It does not assess applicability, which is the threshold question for all three and requires legal analysis.
