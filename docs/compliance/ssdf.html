Scope and limitations

This pack inspects source-controlled artifacts only. It cannot observe build infrastructure, signing key custody, developer training records, or vulnerability disclosure handling. SSDF is unusually amenable to source inspection because it governs the software production process itself. Even so, roughly half of its practices are organizational and are declared undeterminable here rather than omitted. A passing result is evidence toward a practice, never a completed attestation. The CISA secure software development attestation form requires an authorised signatory, not a scanner.
